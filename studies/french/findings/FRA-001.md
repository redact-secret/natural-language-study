# FRA-001: Lowercase particles can belong inside personal names

## Question and scope

How can name-internal particles be distinguished from external prepositions? Scope: Contemporary standard written French; Québec guidance is explicitly scoped; Latn scripts, general correspondence/narrative, PERSON. Original and constructed robustness inputs are distinguished; see [dossier scope](../overview.md).

## Observation

OQLF describes lowercase de/d’ and contracted particles in some personal names, with context-dependent and foreign-name exceptions [FRA-S001](../sources.md#fra-s001). A word that resembles a function word can therefore be name-internal; capitalization alone does not settle membership.

Confidence: **high for the narrowly stated observation**, based on the cited publisher guidance or standard. This confidence does not apply to the NER hypothesis or example interpretations.

## NER hypothesis

Contextual membership decisions for particles will improve multipart-name recall without absorbing external prepositions. Reject if gains are absent or offset by matched-negative errors.
