# POR-004: Registered names and everyday names diverge: Portuguese registration limits versus unofficial Brazilian apelidos

## Question and scope

What naming structures should a PERSON recognizer expect in Portugal and Brazil, and where do registered names and everyday names diverge? Scope: pt-PT registration rules and pt-BR unofficial names; edited prose and social text. Excludes immigrant, Indigenous and other naming communities in Lusophone countries, and legal advice.

## Observation

- Portugal: the official service page states that a name has at most six grammatical words, of which at most two are the proper name, and that names must be Portuguese or adapted to Portuguese ([POR-S002](../sources.md#por-s002)). Names outside the register (for example foreign residents) may not follow this.
- Brazil: a literature review separates names not recorded at the civil registry (apelidos and similar) from registered ones and summarizes work on football-player apelidos and ballot names ([POR-S011](../sources.md#por-s011)). The Brazilian statute on registered names (Lei 6.015/1973 art. 55 as amended by Lei 14.382/2022, [POR-S010](../sources.md#por-s010)) could not be read on the official page, so no statement about its content is relied on.
- Practical consequence: a text can refer to a person by an apelido, a ballot name, a given name alone, or a chain, and these do not follow the registration pattern.

Confidence: **low**. One verified official page, one secondary review, and one unread statute.

## NER hypothesis

A recognizer that assumes the registered name pattern will miss apelido-based mentions and over-extend into following words, which a mononym and short-chain contrast set can reveal. Predicted failure: no error difference between registered-style and apelido-style mentions.

## Evidence handoff

Positive cases: registered chains, given-name-only mentions, apelidos with do/da. Hard negatives: nicknames used as common nouns. Variation: pt-PT versus pt-BR, social versus edited text. Deferred.

## Evaluation handoff

Proposed slice **por-name-forms**: compare registered, short-chain and apelido mentions, holding context fixed. Deferred.

## Implementation options

Baseline: current behavior. Option: allow short mononym spans without a following surname. Tradeoff: precision on common nouns. Options only.

## Limitations and review

Low confidence. Brazil registration law not read. No attested corpus; apelido evidence is a literature-review abstract and introduction. Gender and plural-surname practices are not covered. Review: none recorded; author self-check only.
