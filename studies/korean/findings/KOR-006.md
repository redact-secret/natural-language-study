# KOR-006: 씨 requires a referent decision as well as a spacing decision

## Question and scope

Which assumptions about name form or reference could reject valid mentions or create false positives? Scope follows the [dossier overview](../overview.md); constructed robustness cases are not claims about normal usage.

## Observation

NIKL distinguishes spaced dependent noun 씨 used in respectful reference from attached -씨 denoting a surname or family/clan ([KOR-S007](../sources.md#kor-s007), answer). The target taxonomy excludes 씨 from an individual PERSON span ([KOR-S005](../sources.md#kor-s005), Korean boundary policy). That exclusion cannot decide whether a surname discussion refers to an individual at all.

Observation confidence: medium overall, because source-backed distinctions are narrower than the proposed NER application.

## NER hypothesis

Combining referential context with 씨 attachment will reduce surname-as-category false positives relative to stripping 씨 and accepting every remaining surname.

## Evidence handoff

Pair individual reference, surname discussion, family/clan reference and deliberately missing-space variants. Evidence owners must decide collective-reference policy rather than map all collective readings silently to a negative class.

Disposition: ready-for-owner-review local proposal; no downstream issue created.
