# KOR-002: Name spacing and title spacing are distinct rules

## Question and scope

How can allowed name spacing be handled separately from titles and nonstandard missing spaces? Scope: Contemporary South Korean standard written Korean; Hang, Hani, Latn scripts, general correspondence/narrative, PERSON. Original and constructed robustness inputs are distinguished; see [dossier scope](../overview.md).

## Observation

Article 48 normally joins surname and given name, separates added address terms or official titles, and permits separating surname and given name when clarification is needed [KOR-S001](../sources.md#kor-s001). This is a scoped exception, not optional spacing throughout Korean.

Confidence: **high for the narrowly stated observation**, based on the cited publisher guidance or standard. This confidence does not apply to the NER hypothesis or example interpretations.

## NER hypothesis

Allowing reviewed internal name spaces while modeling titles separately will recover spaced names without swallowing adjacent titles. Reject if gains are absent or offset by matched-negative errors.

## Evidence handoff

Contrast joined/clarified names, surname plus title, title alone and intentionally nonstandard glued titles. Label normative versus noisy variants separately. Destination: `ner-evidence`; **deferred pending language/example and annotation-policy review**. No downstream issue has been opened.
