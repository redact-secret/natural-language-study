# JPN-002: Address suffixes and following particles need separate decisions

## Question and scope

Where should PERSON end before an address suffix and a following particle? Scope: Contemporary standard written Japanese; Hani, Hira, Kana, Latn scripts, general correspondence/narrative, PERSON. Original and constructed robustness inputs are distinguished; see [dossier scope](../overview.md).

## Observation

The Japan Foundation lesson shows a personal name followed by さん and then も [JPN-S002](../sources.md#jpn-s002), L3-9. This teaching example establishes that sequence in a narrow context. UD supplies the broader segmentation distinction [JPN-S001](../sources.md#jpn-s001); excluding an address suffix from PERSON remains an annotation proposal.

Confidence: **medium for the narrowly stated observation**, based on the cited publisher guidance or standard. This confidence does not apply to the NER hypothesis or example interpretations.

## Illustrative examples and contrasts

Examples: **synthetic**, Codex-authored, fictional people, repository MIT license. Text/translation self-check only; qualified language review pending. Candidate spans are proposals, not gold annotations.

| ID | Original text | Meaning | Boundary question / contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ex-01 | `田中さんも来た。` | Tanaka also came (polite address). | Propose 田中; evaluate さん and も over-inclusion independently. |
| ex-02 | `田中も来た。` | Tanaka also came. | Hold the name and particle fixed while removing the address suffix. |
| ex-03 | `みなさんも来た。` | Everyone also came. | The ending さん must not alone establish a personal name. |

## NER hypothesis

Separating name, address suffix and particle roles will reduce boundary over-extension without turning every さん-ending expression into PERSON. Reject if gains are absent or offset by matched-negative errors.

## Evidence handoff

Pair bare and さん forms with the same following particle; include みなさん. Extend to 様, 君 and titles only after dedicated source and example review. Destination: `ner-evidence`; **deferred pending language/example and annotation-policy review**. No downstream issue has been opened.

## Implementation options

Compare contextual suffix features and analyzer features with baseline. Avoid a global さん-stripping pass. Destination: `fastner`; proposed experiments only, deferred until evidence review.

## Limitations and review

Medium confidence: one narrow pedagogical source for address usage; native-language review is missing. This finding does not establish a complete honorific inventory. Independent language review and representative corpus evidence are missing.
