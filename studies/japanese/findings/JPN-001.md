# JPN-001: Segmentation units do not define PERSON spans

## Question and scope

How can a PERSON candidate cross morphological units in unspaced text? Scope: Contemporary standard written Japanese; Hani, Hira, Kana, Latn scripts, general correspondence/narrative, PERSON. Original and constructed robustness inputs are distinguished; see [dossier scope](../overview.md).

## Observation

UD Japanese documents ordinary writing without explicit word spaces and multiple segmentation standards, including short and long units [JPN-S001](../sources.md#jpn-s001). Choosing a morphological unit does not, by itself, choose an entity unit.

Confidence: **high for the narrowly stated observation**, based on the cited publisher guidance or standard. This confidence does not apply to the NER hypothesis or example interpretations.

Examples: **synthetic**, Codex-authored, fictional people, repository MIT license. Text/translation self-check only; qualified language review pending. Candidate spans are proposals, not gold annotations.

## NER hypothesis

Candidates capable of crossing analyzer tokens will improve full-name recall over a one-token name constraint; character candidates may avoid analyzer dictionary misses. Reject if gains are absent or offset by matched-negative errors.
