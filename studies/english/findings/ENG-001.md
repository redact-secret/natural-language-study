# ENG-001: Apostrophes inside names versus attached clitics

## Question and scope

How can internal name apostrophes be retained while external clitics are excluded? Scope: Contemporary edited British and American English; Latn scripts, general correspondence/narrative, PERSON. Original and constructed robustness inputs are distinguished; see [dossier scope](../overview.md).

## Observation

Possessive ’s and contracted auxiliaries are attached forms [ENG-S001](../sources.md#eng-s001); UD separates clitics as syntactic words [ENG-S004](../sources.md#eng-s004). Names can also contain punctuation [ENG-S003](../sources.md#eng-s003). Our inference: the presence of an apostrophe alone cannot select a PERSON boundary.

Confidence: **high for the narrowly stated observation**, based on the cited publisher guidance or standard. This confidence does not apply to the NER hypothesis or example interpretations.

## NER hypothesis

Contextual apostrophe roles will reduce name truncation relative to splitting every apostrophe, without increasing false positives on possessive common nouns. Reject if gains are absent or offset by matched-negative errors.
